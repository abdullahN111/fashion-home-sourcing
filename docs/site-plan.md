# Site Plan & Information Architecture

## Target Pages (5-6 Page Setup)
1. `/` (Home): Hero with high-impact textile imagery, Core Pillars (Quality, Sourcing, Sustainability), Product Highlights, Metrics Banner (5K+ Projects, 20+ Years, 99% Satisfaction), Client CTA.
2. `/about` (About Us): Heritage, ethical manufacturing, global export reach, quality assurance standards, and core values, Supplier details with certifications or logos.
3. `/products` (All Products): Category-filtered gallery (Work Wear, Woven, Finished Fabric, Home Textiles, Technical Textiles) with construction details (GSM, thread counts, fiber blends) and Supplier details with certifications or logos.
4. `/products/[category]` (Optional dedicated category or anchored deep-dive on `/products`).
5. `/sustainability` or `/quality`: Compliance, certifications (OEKO-TEX, GOTS, ISO), supply-chain integrity, and testing lab standards.
6. `/contact` (Contact Us): RFQ (Request for Quote) inquiry form, factory location, business email, and direct WhatsApp/phone contact info.

## Footer Structure (Locked)
- Links:
  1. Home (`/`)
  2. Products (`/products`)
  3. About (`/about`)
  4. Contact (`/contact`)
- Contact Info: Corporate email, factory/office location, and business hours.
- Bottom bar: Clean copyright notice.
